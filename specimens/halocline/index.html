Coastal groundwater group

Where does fresh water go when the sea pushes in?

Under much of the Dutch coast, fresh groundwater floats on top of salt. We measure where the boundary sits, model how it moves, and work with farmers and water managers on what to do about it.

Research

Three lines of work, one moving boundary.

We work at three scales at once: wells in the field, models of the whole coast, and decisions on individual farms.

Field · since 2024

Salt under farmland

A network of monitoring wells across three coastal polders, logging salinity every hour. The longest record of its kind in the region.

2 PhD candidates · 1 technician
Models · since 2025

A coast in motion

Groundwater models that run decades ahead under different sea-level and drought scenarios, tested against the well data.

1 postdoc · 1 PhD candidate
Practice · since 2026

Living with salt

Working with farmers and water managers on irrigation, drainage and salt-tolerant crops, and on which measures are worth the cost.

1 PhD candidate · partners in the field
