What you'll learn

Four methods,
your own material.

Every afternoon you apply the morning's method to sources you bring: letters, newspapers, registers, interviews.

01

Text mining

Search and count across thousands of digitised pages, and know what the counts can and can't tell you.

02

Networks

Turn correspondence or membership lists into networks, and read them critically.

03

Maps

Place people, events and records on historical maps.

04

AI, carefully

Where language models help with transcription and tagging, and where they quietly get things wrong.
