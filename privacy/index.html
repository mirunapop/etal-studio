Privacy

DRAFT — to review. This text has not been checked yet and may change before it is final.

The short version: this website sets no cookies, has no forms and runs no analytics. If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use it to reply to you.

Who is responsible

Myra Pop, trading as Et al. Studio, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. The only way to contact me is by email: hello@etal.studio.

What this website does

  • It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • It has no forms. Nothing you type on a page is sent anywhere.
  • It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels.
  • Fonts, scripts and images all come from etal.studio itself. Your browser does not contact Google or any other third party to show a page.
  • The Copy buttons use your browser's clipboard only when you press them. Nothing is sent.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in their server logs. I run no analytics of my own on top of that. GitHub explains how it handles this in its own privacy statement.

If you send me an email

I receive your email address, your name if it is in the address or signature, and what you choose to write. I use it to answer you and, if we work together, to prepare a quote and run the project. I do not add you to a mailing list and I do not sell or pass on your details. The messages are stored by my email provider.

I do this because you asked me something or, once we work together, because it is needed for the contract. Quotes, order confirmations and invoices are kept for as long as Dutch tax law requires. Other emails are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it or delete it, or object to how I use it. Email me and I will reply within a month at the latest. If you are not happy with my answer, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
