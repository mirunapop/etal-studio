Privacy

This website sets no cookies, has no forms and runs no analytics. If you email me, I use what you send to reply to you.

This website

It stores nothing in your browser and loads nothing from other companies: the fonts, scripts and images all come from etal.studio itself.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in server logs. I don't see them and run no tracking of my own. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles this.

Email

If you write to hello@etal.studio, I receive your email address and whatever you include. I use it to reply and, if we work together, to run the project. I don't share it or add you to a mailing list. I keep it as long as needed for that, and invoices for as long as Dutch tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Email me and I will reply within a month. You can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, the Dutch data protection authority.
