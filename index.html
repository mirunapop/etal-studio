1Method

One person, start to finish

The person who designs your site also writes the code, tests it and launches it. Nothing gets lost between an account manager and a developer.

Built, not assembled

No page builder, no forty plugins, no theme licence that lapses in year three. Your team can still edit the news, people and publications.

Checked before you see it

Built to WCAG 2.2 AA and tested on phones and with screen readers. EU-hosted, with no cookie banner needed.

Yours to keep

The code, domain and hosting are in your institute's name. When the grant ends, the site can be frozen into an archive that won't break.

4Supplementary

Frequently asked questions

Can it live on our university's domain?

Often, yes, as a subdomain or a path, if your IT department allows it. Otherwise the project gets its own domain, registered in your institute's name.

Who updates the site after launch?

Your team does: news, people and publications can be edited without touching code. Or Care covers small changes within two working days.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes, for the scope in the order confirmation. Anything extra is quoted before any work starts.

What happens when the grant ends?

The site is frozen into a static archive: every page and link keeps working, with nothing left to update or hack. It's included with Care, or quoted at the start so it can go in the budget.
